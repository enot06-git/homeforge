# HomeForge

## Product Purpose
Personal home gym workout tracker. Logs sessions, plans next-session weights using RIR (Reps in Reserve) data, tracks mesocycles, and syncs to Google Sheets. Single-user, private tool.

## Register
product

## Users
One user: Evgenii, 49yo intermediate lifter, 83kg/176cm, 6 years training. Uses the app on mobile (phone) during gym sessions. Needs fast logging with minimal taps. No onboarding needed — he built it.

## Brand Tone
Functional, dense, zero fluff. Dark gym aesthetic. Data-first. No marketing language.

## Anti-references
- No fitness app clichés (motivational quotes, streaks, confetti, green ticks)
- No SaaS dashboard hero metrics
- No white/light themes

## Strategic Principles
- Every tap counts — this is used mid-workout with sweaty hands
- Data accuracy over visual polish
- Mobile-first (used on phone, not desktop)
- Offline-capable (localStorage primary, Sheets as backup)
